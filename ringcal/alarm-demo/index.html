A real scheduled reminder, its ringing screen, ongoing notification and Dismiss.

This uncut 45-second native screen recording uses the normal Play test build 21 on an Android 16 emulator. It starts with a new reminder’s time picker open, then shows its title, Morning Bell sound, saved one-minute ring limit, Save, returning Home, the screen asleep, the due alarm, notification actions and Dismiss.

This recording does not capture alarm audio. It demonstrates the interface and scheduled flow. Audible long built-in/custom sound, Snooze and Dismiss have been tested separately on the selected Galaxy Fold. This video does not establish every phone, call or audio-route condition.

No app interface is reconstructed or sound track substituted. The original video frames are preserved; recorder metadata is removed. The selected alarm is a publishing example. The Play edition remains unpublished.

To try the same flow: open Calendar → Add reminder, enter an example title, select a near-future time and sound, choose a saved ring limit, then Save. First review precise-alarm and notification access in RingCal’s alarm setup. Full-screen access controls presentation; notification actions are the fallback. Check your alarm volume, Do Not Disturb and sound route.
