RingCal — Google Play edition privacy policy
Effective October 4, 2026

Developer: Kamal Ahmed
Contact: kamalacca@gmail.com

What stays on your device

RingCal stores reminder titles, notes, dates, repeat rules, alarm settings, delivery history and preferences locally on your Android device. A custom sound choice stores a reference to the file you select and its access grant. RingCal does not upload these records or the selected audio to a server.

Data collection and sharing

This edition of RingCal has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect or share your data off-device. It has no Internet permission and does not check GitHub for updates. Google Play manages app downloads and updates under Google's own policies. Android and a file provider you choose may process information under their own policies; a provider may fetch a cloud-backed file you selected.

Permissions and notifications

Notification, precise-alarm and optional full-screen access are used to deliver the reminders you create. A temporary foreground service, wake lock and vibration support a due alarm for its saved duration. Choosing an audio document grants access to that chosen file; RingCal does not request broad access to your media library. Notification and lock-screen content can be visible to others according to your device settings.

Retention, deletion and backup

Reminders and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app's storage. RingCal may automatically remove obsolete completed scheduling and delivery history once it is more than 90 days old and belongs to an earlier reminder revision. Dismissing an alarm is not the same as deleting its reminder or all local history. Android Settings can clear all RingCal storage; uninstalling the app also removes its local data. RingCal disables Android application backup and device-transfer backup for these records. There is no RingCal cloud backup or server copy to recover after removal.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, Kamal and his email provider receive the information you send. RingCal does not automatically attach reminders, files or diagnostics. Send only the details needed to handle your question. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request; contact the email above about deletion or privacy questions.

Policy changes

If RingCal's handling of information changes, the policy will be updated with the relevant release. This policy covers the Google Play edition. The GitHub edition has a separate policy describing its GitHub update checks.
