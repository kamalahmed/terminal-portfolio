Plan it. Hear it. Carry on.

One less thing
to keep in your head.

From watering the plants to taking a stretch break, RingCal brings your reminders and alarm choices together in one calm calendar. Choose a day, add the details and decide how you want to be reminded.

01

Start with a day.

Add a title, time and notes. See what’s ahead in the calendar or search your upcoming reminders.

02

Make it a rhythm.

Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. Complete one occurrence and keep the next, or end the repeating task.

03

Choose how it rings.

Preview a built-in, Android or custom sound. Set sound repetition, ring duration, vibration and snooze.

Your reminders stay with you

On your phone.
In your hands.

RingCal stores reminders and preferences on your device. The calendar and reminders work offline.

Read the Play edition’s privacy policy ↗
  • No account to create.
  • No advertising or analytics.
  • Choose Light, Dark or your system appearance.
  • Use a 12-hour or 24-hour clock.

Clearing storage or uninstalling removes your local reminders. There is no RingCal cloud backup.
