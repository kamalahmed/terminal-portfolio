Common questions

How do I check alarm setup?

Open RingCal’s alarm setup or reliability check. Review notification and precise-alarm access, alarm volume, Do Not Disturb and the phone’s background restrictions. Use “Try an alarm,” then create a short test reminder and check it with the phone locked.

Full-screen access controls the lock-screen presentation. It does not guarantee that audio will work. The beta is still undergoing device compatibility testing.

The notification appears, but the alarm is silent.

Check the alarm volume and the selected sound preview. If RingCal reports “Audio focus is unavailable,” include that exact message in your email. Preview playback alone does not establish that a scheduled background alarm will play. Do not rely on a silent test alarm.

Where can I download RingCal?

The experimental beta is available from RingCal’s GitHub releases page. The free Google Play edition is in preparation and is not published yet. The GitHub beta supports Android 8.0 or later; the planned Play edition supports Android 15 or later.

Can I recover reminders after uninstalling?

RingCal has no cloud backup. Uninstalling or clearing its storage removes local reminders and preferences. A compatible in-place update is different from uninstalling; use the release’s update instructions.

Why are there two privacy policies?

The Google Play edition policy covers the edition with Play-managed updates. The GitHub edition policy also explains the app’s GitHub update checks.

Support is by email. This page has no contact form, tracking scripts or automatic diagnostic upload. Hosting providers process ordinary website requests under their own policies.
