RingCal — beta app preview

Make room for your day with RingCal. A calm calendar for the things you want to remember.

Choose a day and time. Add a title and notes, so the details are there when you need them.

Make reminders repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Complete one occurrence and keep the next.

Choose an alarm sound you'll recognize. Preview built-in sounds, Android tones, or your own audio.

Your reminders stay on your device. No sign-in, ads, or analytics. RingCal. Your calendar. Your reminders.

The free Google Play edition is in preparation for Android 15 or later.
